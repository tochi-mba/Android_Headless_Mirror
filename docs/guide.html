Connect your phone the first time

Once only. After this the mirror opens by itself whenever the phone is plugged in.

  1. Install the app and open it. The window shows the phone side step by step.
  2. On the phone, open Settings → About phone and tap Build number seven times.
  3. Open Developer options and turn on USB debugging.
  4. Plug the phone in with a cable that carries data. Unlock it, tick Always allow from this computer and tap Allow.
  5. The mirror opens and the phone's own screen turns off. Tap its power button any time to use it in your hand.
Settings
Open the window when a phone connects, Phone screen off while mirroring

Go fullscreen and keep the controls

Fullscreen fills the whole display; the controls shrink to a small bar you place yourself.

  1. Press F11.
  2. Move the pointer: the controls fade in. Drag the bar by its dotted grip to wherever suits you.
  3. Leave it alone and it fades. Move the pointer back to where you left it and it returns.
  4. Press Esc to put the window back.
Keys
F11, Esc

Zoom in and move around

Zoom scales the picture on this PC; the phone does not know.

  1. Hold Alt and turn the mouse wheel, or pinch with two fingers on the touchpad.
  2. Hold Alt and drag, or slide two fingers, to move around.
  3. Drag the frame in the navigator to jump to another part of the screen.
  4. Ctrl+Alt+0 fits the phone to the window again.
Keys
Alt + wheel, Alt + pinch, Alt + drag, Ctrl+Alt+0

Keep the phone's music quieter than the PC, or mute it from anywhere

This changes the phone's sound on this PC only; the phone's own volume stays where it is.

  1. Click the sound button in the top bar and drag the slider. The level is kept for this phone.
  2. Or press Ctrl+Alt+PageDown and Ctrl+Alt+PageUp; Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M mutes and unmutes.
  3. To mute it from any app, open Settings → Shortcuts from anywhere, choose Mute on this PC and give it a key.
  4. For quiet while you work, turn on Mute while another window is in front in Settings → Sound on this PC.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+PageUp, Ctrl+Alt+PageDown, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+M
Settings
Sound on this PC

Open an app from the PC, and keep favourites on keys

The list comes from the phone itself the first time it is mirrored; Read again fetches it after you install something.

  1. Press Ctrl+Alt+2 for the Apps tab, or click Apps in the side panel.
  2. Start typing a name: the list narrows as you type. Enter or a click opens the app; Shift opens it fresh.
  3. Click the star beside an app to make it a favourite. Favourites have tiles at the top of the Controls tab.
  4. Press Ctrl+Alt+Shift+1 for the first favourite, up to Ctrl+Alt+Shift+9. Move them up and down from their menu to change which key opens which.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+2, Ctrl+Alt+Shift+1 to 9

Send files, folders or an app to the phone

Files go only into the phone's shared storage. APK files are installed instead of copied when that setting is on.

  1. Drop files or a folder onto the window, or choose Send files… in Controls.
  2. To reuse File Explorer's clipboard, copy the files and press Ctrl+Alt+V.
  3. If an APK or a large batch needs approval, check the question and choose Install or Send.
  4. Watch the item in Controls. Cancel stops its own ADB process and removes a partial copy; Try again puts it back in the queue.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+V

Watch a video beside a chat, or use an app at PC size

The second screen is one app on a display of its own. It needs Android 10 or later.

  1. In Controls, choose Open an app on a second screen…, then pick the app in the Apps tab. Or press Ctrl+Alt+D.
  2. Click the view you want to use: keys and Home, Back and Recents go to it, and an outline shows which it is.
  3. Drag the splitter between them to share the space differently; double-click it to put it back.
  4. For the phone's screen to stay dark while the app runs here, choose Instead of it in the same section.
  5. Switch app… puts another app on the same screen; Close it moves the app back to the phone.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+D

A profile for games, and one that switches on by itself

A profile holds only the settings you put in it, so applying one never undoes the rest.

  1. Set the mirror up the way you want it for games: frame rate, bit rate, sound.
  2. In Settings, Profiles, choose Save the current settings as a profile…, name it Gaming, and keep only the groups it is about.
  3. Apply it from its card, from the tray's Profiles menu, or with Ctrl+Alt+F1 while it is first in the list.
  4. To have it on whenever you go fullscreen, choose it under In fullscreen use. Leaving fullscreen puts back what it changed.
  5. On a laptop, On battery use can pick Battery saver the same way.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+F1 to Ctrl+Alt+F9
Command line
rex profile apply Gaming

Type, and browse a feed without the mouse

Typing goes to the phone as it would from a plugged-in keyboard.

  1. Click the phone's picture, then type. Digits, symbols and AltGr work in your phone's keyboard layout.
  2. To scroll a feed with the keyboard, press Ctrl+Alt+K: browse mode is on.
  3. Use Down and Up for the next and previous item, Enter to tap, L to like, M to mute and Backspace to go back.
  4. Press Esc to go back to typing.

Bring the mirror up from anywhere, and give an action a key

These keys work while the window is hidden in the tray or behind other windows.

  1. Press Ctrl+Alt+M in any app: the mirror comes to the front, ready to type into. Press it again and it goes back to the tray.
  2. To choose another key, open Settings → Shortcuts from anywhere, click the box under Show or hide the window and press the keys you want. Backspace turns it off; Esc keeps the old one.
  3. To skip to the next video while you work, pick Next item under More shortcuts from anywhere, choose Add a shortcut… and press a key such as Ctrl+Shift+F9.
  4. Turn on Say what a shortcut did, from the tray to hear back while the window is hidden.

See the phone twice or more

Copies are extra live views of the same phone, side by side.

  1. Hold the phone upright.
  2. Press Ctrl+Alt+N, or Add a copy in Controls. Repeat for more, as many as fit the window at full height.
  3. Touch, type and zoom in any of them.
  4. Ctrl+Alt+W closes the last copy.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+N, Ctrl+Alt+W

Unlock a phone whose lock screen is black

Some phones protect their lock screen from being mirrored. The guide shows where the dots are.

  1. Lock the phone. The first time, the app asks what kind of lock it has; choose Pattern.
  2. The nine-dot guide appears over the black picture, where Android puts the dots.
  3. Draw your pattern over it with the mouse.
  4. If the dots are off, press Ctrl+Alt+C and line them up with the arrow keys.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+P, Ctrl+Alt+C
Settings
Lock screen

Take screenshots and record

Screenshots are saved in full quality; recordings capture each session.

  1. Press Ctrl+Alt+S, or the camera in the top bar.
  2. The status bar names the file. Click the name to show it in File Explorer.
  3. To record every session, turn on Record every session in Settings.
Keys
Ctrl+Alt+S

Fix a phone Windows will not read

When Windows binds the wrong driver or gives up reading the phone, the app notices within a few seconds.

  1. Leave the phone plugged in. The bar above the mirror offers Repair USB driver or Fix USB.
  2. Click it and approve the Windows administrator prompt once.
  3. To have it fixed without asking next time, choose Fix automatically from now on.
  4. Still nothing? Try a port on the PC itself rather than a hub, and another cable.
