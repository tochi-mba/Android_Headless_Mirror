Shortcuts

Every key, and what it does.

This list is made from the app's own, so it always matches the version on the download button.

In the window

They work while the app's window is in front, whatever has the keyboard. Most are Ctrl+Alt with a key; AltGr is never taken, so it still types on the phone.

KeysWhat happens
F11Fill the display
EscLeave fullscreen
F1Show the tour again
Ctrl+Alt+BShow or hide the side panel
Ctrl+Alt+1The Controls tab
Ctrl+Alt+2The Phone tab
Ctrl+Alt+3The Settings tab
Ctrl+Alt+4The Info tab
Ctrl+Alt+HHome
Ctrl+Alt+BackspaceBack
Ctrl+Alt+RRecent apps
Ctrl+Alt+UpPrevious item in a feed (swipe down)
Ctrl+Alt+DownNext item in a feed (swipe up)
Ctrl+Alt+EnterTap the centre: open, play or pause
Ctrl+Alt+KBrowse mode on or off: plain keys drive the phone
Ctrl+Alt+NAdd a copy of the phone beside it
Ctrl+Alt+WRemove the last copy
Ctrl+Alt+SSave a screenshot
Ctrl+Alt+PlusZoom the PC view in
Ctrl+Alt+MinusZoom the PC view out
Ctrl+Alt+0Fit the phone to the window
Ctrl+Alt+ULock the phone to portrait
Ctrl+Alt+LLock the phone to landscape
Ctrl+Alt+ALet the phone rotate by itself
Ctrl+Alt+LeftTurn the PC view left
Ctrl+Alt+RightTurn the PC view right
Ctrl+Alt+PShow or hide the pattern guide
Ctrl+Alt+CCalibrate the guide with the arrow keys
Ctrl+Alt+PageUpThe phone's sound louder on this PC
Ctrl+Alt+PageDownThe phone's sound quieter on this PC
Ctrl+Alt+Shift+MMute or unmute the phone's sound on this PC

From anywhere

They work while the window is hidden in the tray or behind other windows, as long as the app runs. The key is yours to choose in Settings, Shortcuts from anywhere, where any action can have a key of its own too.

KeysWhat happens
Ctrl+Alt+MShow or hide the window, from anywhere

Browse mode

Press Ctrl+Alt+K first. While browse mode is on, these plain keys act on the phone instead of typing into it; letters still type, so a search box works. Esc goes back to typing.

KeysWhat happens
DownNext item in a feed (swipe up)
UpPrevious item in a feed (swipe down)
RightNext story, photo or page (swipe left)
LeftPrevious story, photo or page (swipe right)
EnterTap the centre: open, play or pause (Space does the same)
LLike: double-tap the centre
MMute or unmute the phone
BackspaceBack

Mouse and touchpad

Two fingers on a precision touchpad are two real fingers on the phone. Hold Alt and the same gestures move the view on this PC instead, and the phone receives nothing.

KeysWhat happens
Alt + wheelZoom the PC view at the pointer
Alt + pinchZoom the PC view on a touchpad
Alt + dragPan while zoomed
Two fingersPinch, rotate and pan on the phone
